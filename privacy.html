Daily Digest

Privacy policy

Last updated October 13, 2026

Who uses it

Daily Digest is used by one person, its owner. It runs on the owner’s Mac and on devices the owner pairs with that Mac over a private network.

What it accesses from Google

Only two Google Calendar permissions:

  • See the list of the owner’s calendars.
  • See and edit events on the owner’s calendars.

It does not ask for Gmail, Contacts, Drive or any other Google data.

How it uses that data

To show the owner’s events inside the app, and to create, change or delete events when the owner asks it to.

Where it is kept

The Google sign-in and the calendar events are kept on the owner’s Mac, on the owner’s paired devices, and in the owner’s own backups. The app does not send them to any other company or service.

Sharing

Google user data is not shared, sold or transferred to anyone, and is not used for advertising. Daily Digest’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Disconnect Google Calendar inside the app on the Mac: that revokes the app’s access with Google and deletes the sign-in from the Mac. Access can also be removed at any time from your Google Account’s third-party connections.

Contact

Questions go to the support email shown on Google’s sign-in screen for Daily Digest.

Daily Digest

Daily Digest is a personal app that runs on its owner’s own Mac. It brings the owner’s tasks, notes, reading and calendar into one page, and keeps working offline on the owner’s own devices.

It is not a public service. There is no sign-up and no account to create.

Google Calendar

With the owner’s permission, Daily Digest reads the owner’s list of calendars and reads and edits events on them. That lets the day’s schedule appear beside the owner’s tasks, and lets a task be booked as a calendar event.
